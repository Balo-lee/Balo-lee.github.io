About Me

I'm Muhibbullah Balogun, an aspiring Software Engineer built through consistent, hands-on practice rather than a classroom. After completing WAEC in 2025 and JAMB in 2026, I applied to the Software Engineering program at the Federal University of Technology, Minna — and while I wait, I keep building. HTML and CSS gave me a way to turn ideas into real, working interfaces; now I'm deepening my JavaScript to make those interfaces do more.

I care about writing clean, semantic code and building things that actually work well on any screen — not just look good in a screenshot. Every project on this site was built end-to-end by me, mistakes and all, because that's how I actually learn.
